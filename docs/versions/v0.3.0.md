# v0.3.0: the story campaign

Branch `v0.3.0`; its version pull request into `main` is #93. The design is `docs/design/story-v0.3.0.md` (approved by
the team on 15 November 2026). What is done is in the branch's history (`tools/dev/state.sh` lists the integrated
steps); what is open is in the GitHub issues of milestone v0.3.0. This page is the plan that ties them together.

## What a player gets

1. **The story: the cuyes who were there.** Four cuy astronauts (Rumi, Killa, Inti, Wayra) slipped into six real NASA
   missions, played in the order they happened, every fact from a NASA page. A prologue in the Andes, then each
   chapter as an intro film, a mission played by its own rules, and an outro.
2. **Every mission also on its own**, from the Missions screen, without the story (team decision, #43).
3. **The crew of friends**: up to four players on one screen or online, each at a station (#51), and the ranking (#52).

## How we work on it

The method for every agent on this version is in issue #62 (play as a stranger, judge, patch at the cause, iterate)
and in `AGENTS.md`. A new mission waits until every mechanic it uses is right (`AGENTS.md`, "Mechanics before
content").
