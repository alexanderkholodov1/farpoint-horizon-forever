General objective: create an interactive game that allows participants to design, manage, and simulate a complete space mission while making engineering decisions, managing limited resources, and evaluating how each choice shapes the success of their mission.

Specific objective:

Designing a space mission:
- consideration of competing demands
- mission objectives
- spacecraft design
- scientific instruments
- launch vehicles
- budgets
- power
- mass
- communications
- orbital constraints



"scientific objectives, engineering requirements, available resources, and operational constraints must work together".

"A decision that improves one aspect of a mission can create limitations in another. For example, adding a more capable instrument may increase scientific value while also increasing mass, power consumption, data volume, and cost" - dar objetivos a elegir antes de lanzar la mision, que daran puntos a la partida final, que requieren ciertos instrumentos y estos instrumentos deben obtener la informacion necesaria y enviarla, para obtener estos puntos. 

"individually or collaborate in teams of up to four people, with each participant contributing to the mission strategy and working together to achieve the best possible result" - diferentes roles, tanto que se quedan en la Tierra, cosmonautas que van al espacio, y mas, y todos deben colaborar a nivel de mecanicas como en el juego We Were Here. "Team members could have different responsibilities, such as mission planning, spacecraft design, science, communications, or resource management." - si juegas con varias personas, podrias tener un lobby como en Don't Starve Together, donde cada uno podria escoger su "personaje" (rol en la mision, basado en la vida real, pero simplificado para funcionar con 4 personas). Tambien, si hay otros equipos participando a la vez en la pagina, debes poder verlos de lejos, y verlos despegar, sin que molesten a tu propio gameplay, pero que te permita ver que hay otros usuarios jugando.

"Potential approaches could include mission planning, spacecraft configuration, instrument selection, resource management, orbital selection, launch planning, communications, and mission simulation" - todo esto.

"A strong solution might include a functional interactive game or simulation with a clear user interface, mission configuration system, resource management mechanisms, collaboration or multiplayer functionality for up to four participants, and a mission simulation and evaluation system".

"Clear objectives and feedback help participants understand why a mission succeeds or fails." - aparte de eso, un game design y prograso en el juego, donde el lanzar una mision se siente impactante, y el final del juego daria el climax, con musica y mas.
Team notes (lead), 2026-11-14:
- The idea comes out of a design round: the brigadista guides and aligns, the human criterion defines the central idea.
- Deadline: everything frozen by 07:00 on Sunday 2026-11-15; no fixed time slots before that.
- We know Godot but do not master it: prepare agents and tools able to operate Godot (MCP servers, screen control, tests). The teammate's work gets the same support as the lead's.
- 3D, with the complete mission as in Kerbal: build the rocket or craft in a builder, plan the orbit, reach orbit, take it to the target, and land.
- From Kerbal we do NOT take the fail-and-retry loop or its difficulty. What inspires us in Kerbal: to be written from the lead's answer.
- Godot, optimized for the web from the start (final call after seeing a working demo).
- Firebase (the lead's account) for online multiplayer and hosting; the Firebase project is created once the brainstorm names the game.
- Disclosure: cite every source we use (information, formulas, data) and name the AI agents used in a disclosure section. No watermarks.
- The teammate's sketch is about 70% there and is our solid base. It needs a stronger plot and game design, well-made 3D models, and a more unique UI.
- Several mission types: yes.
- Audience: the general public. One complete, satisfying mission, not a 5-minute minigame, and simpler than Kerbal.
- Main takeaway for the player: there is no perfect design.
- The game's name is decided in the brainstorm.

Team notes (lead), 2026-11-14, on the vision and the base:
- Roles differ: a pilot does depend on reflexes and control panels.
- Nothing should feel like a lost opportunity or a failure; the focus is fun and the satisfaction of your work, shown through sound effects, animation and effects. The feeling to aim for: wonder and the pride of watching your own craft lift off, deploy, arrive and land.
- Blowing up should be possible, hard for a normal player to cause by accident, and fun for anyone who does it on purpose.
- Do not follow the sketch blindly: varied missions. Launching from Ecuador, yes, plus international and even fictional missions (Mars, the Moon, Venus).
- More missions to choose from, and more entertaining. Besides the mission, bonuses: extra objectives (for example, information only a special instrument can get) and success in your role (doing something well adds points for the team; not reaching it never feels bad, reaching a lot feels great). The mission plus extra objectives, chosen among random ones offered.
- Nothing against other teams: you can see other teams, watch them try and see a leaderboard, but nobody competes to win.
- What inspires us in Kerbal: the builder, route and orbit planning, the whole journey to the end, the freedom to invent, the humor (friendly, satisfying, and funny together), and the spectacle of launch and landing.
- Missions that come back and missions that never do (like Voyager). Communication and signal reception matter a lot: for example, a player on Earth follows the mission from the control center and the map and plays mini-games reading the data while others do other work.
- Failures can appear in flight and need solving on the way, as has happened in history; they give extra points and are a source of mini-games.
- Only a person merges (the lead or the teammate), never agents; agents prepare one pull request with the next functional version.
- The teammate researched connecting Blender to the pipeline; integrate it if 3D models need it.
- Versions: v0.1.0 was the first playable sketch; steps 0.1.1, 0.1.2, ... lead to v0.2.0, the second version with everything corrected.
- The base game is done but is still a sketch far from perfect: everything needs adjusting. The design criteria (nothing that looks AI-made) still apply.
