# NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

Team **SK-Horizons** (Quito, Ecuador), challenge **Space Mission Design Game**, project **Farpoint HORIZON**, at the
local event in Sangolquí (Universidad de las Fuerzas Armadas ESPE), 14 and 15 November 2026. We brought no template
and no code: the work began with a brainstorm at the venue and the repository's history starts there, on 14 November
at 09:37 (Ecuador time).
